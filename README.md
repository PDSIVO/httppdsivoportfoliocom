# Remix of PDSIVO Portfolio

Create a modern, professional portfolio website for Israel Victor Olamide (PDSIVO), a Nigerian graphics designer with 3+ years of experience. The brand focuses on clean visual communication, bold brand identity work, and digital design solutions that help businesses look credible and stand out.

The website should feel sleek, modern, and confident, with a design style that reflects a creative but professional designer who works across branding, social media, UI/UX, and print.

Include a high-quality profile photo section (hero image or about section portrait). The image should be well-lit, clean background, and visually aligned with a professional creative identity.

Design System

Color Palette

Primary background: deep navy / charcoal (soft black tone, not pure black)

Accent color: electric blue (#2F80FF style)

Secondary colors: white + light grey

Typography

Modern sans-serif (clean, geometric feel)

Strong hierarchy for headings

Light spacing for readability

UI Style

Minimalist, structured layout

Smooth transitions and hover effects

Subtle glow accents using electric blue

Rounded but sharp-edged balance (not too soft, not too rigid)

Overall Feel

Professional creative studio vibe

High-end but not overly flashy

Designed to attract clients, not just showcase art

Final Instruction

The website must position PDSIVO as a serious, modern brand designer capable of handling real business identity and digital design work, with a strong focus on clarity, trust, and visual impact.
